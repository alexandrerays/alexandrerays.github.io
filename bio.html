Bio

Alexandre Ray is an data scientist, ai engineer and entrepreneur with over ten years of experience leading data and artificial intelligence initiatives across multiple industries, including finance, consulting, healthcare, and manufacturing.

He is the Co-Founder and CTO of OncoAI, a healthtech company using AI to predict cancer recurrence and personalize medical treatment.

Throughout his career, Alexandre has worked with global companies such as Mars and EY, as well as high-growth startups.

He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Computational Physics and a Master’s degree in Computer Engineering from the University of São Paulo (USP), with international academic experience at Tel Aviv University.

Passionate about innovation and the future of technology, Alexandre focuses on transforming complex challenges into scalable, high-impact solutions that create value for both businesses and society.
